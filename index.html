What do I need to earn from here on?

Use this at any point in the semester. It tells you how many of the remaining course points you need for each letter grade.

  1. In Canvas, open BIO-107 and click Grades in the left menu.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the page to the Total row.
  3. Enter the two numbers shown there (for example, 234.80 / 327.50): points earned on the left, points earnable on the right.

What do I need on the final exam?

Use this once every other assignment has been graded and only the final exam remains (900 points have been earnable; the final is worth 100 points).

  1. In Canvas, open BIO-107 and click Grades in the left menu.
  2. At the top right of the page, find Total: __.__%.
  3. Enter that percentage below.
